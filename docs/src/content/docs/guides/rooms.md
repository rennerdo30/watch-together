title: Watching in a room
description: Join a room, add videos and adjust playback.
---

## Join or create a room

Open your Watch Together instance. If its operator uses Cloudflare Access,
sign in with an account allowed by that operator. Use **Create room** on the
home page, or open a room link someone shares with you.

Room membership and available actions depend on the instance's authentication
and the room's permissions. A room's admin controls its shared settings.

## Add videos

Paste a video URL into the room's URL input and add it to the queue. The server
resolves the source and shares the resulting playback state with the room.
Use the room UI to manage its queue; playlist import is available to moderators.

For a video that needs a signed-in session, connect the
[browser extension](../extension/). Sign in to the original video site in the
same browser. Availability still depends on the video site's access rules and
whether Watch Together can resolve the source.

## Playback controls

Playback position is synchronized through the server. Playback controls apply
according to the room's permissions. Your volume, audio output and local
enhancement preferences are separate from the room's shared playback position.

When a source offers multiple audio or quality choices, use the player controls
to choose them. Live streams show a live indicator instead of a normal video
timeline. Player settings also include audio normalization, mono audio and
optional video enhancement.

## Keep the room connected

Leave the room tab open while watching. The connected extension helps keep
Chrome from discarding open room tabs. Browser suspension, sleep and network
interruptions can still disconnect a client; return to the tab to reconnect.

## Cookies and watch history

The extension can lend your signed-in session to video resolution for other
members of the room. Read the [privacy policy](../../privacy/) for the scope and
retention of this data. YouTube watch-history reporting is a separate opt-in
setting and is off by default.
