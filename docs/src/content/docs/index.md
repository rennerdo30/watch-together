Watch Together synchronizes video playback for people in the same room. Share
a room link, add videos to its queue, and watch together in your browser.

## Start watching

- [Watching in a room](./guides/rooms/): join a room, add videos and choose playback settings.
- [Browser extension](./guides/extension/): installation, connection, cookie sync and your controls.
- [Project overview](./overview/): supported features and the project structure.

## Run an instance

Follow the [deployment guide](./hosting/overview/) for setup and the
[production operations guide](./hosting/production/) for the Cloudflare Tunnel
deployment, verified identity, DNS and troubleshooting.

## Privacy and support

The extension handles login cookies and sends them to the instance you connect
to. Read the [extension privacy policy](./privacy/) before connecting.

Privacy questions: [support@renner.dev](mailto:support@renner.dev).
For bugs, use the [project's issue tracker](https://github.com/rennerdo30/watch-together/issues).
